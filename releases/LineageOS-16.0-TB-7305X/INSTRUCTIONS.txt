================================================================================
  Lenovo Tab M7 (TB-7305X) LineageOS 16.0 & TWRP 3.3.1 Installation Guide
================================================================================

PREREQUISITES:
1. Charge your tablet to at least 50%.
2. Backup all personal data (installing custom ROM will completely wipe the tablet).
3. Have Android Platform Tools (adb & fastboot) installed on your PC.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: UNLOCK THE BOOTLOADER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. In the tablet Settings -> About Tablet -> Tap "Build Number" 7 times.
2. In Settings -> System -> Developer Options:
   - Turn ON "OEM Unlocking"
   - Turn ON "USB Debugging"
3. Connect the tablet to your computer via USB cable.
4. Open Command Prompt or Terminal on your PC and run:
     adb reboot bootloader
5. Once your tablet screen shows "FASTBOOT mode...", run:
     fastboot flashing unlock
6. On the tablet, press the Volume Up button to confirm the unlock.
   (The tablet will format data and reboot).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: FLASH LINEAGEOS 16.0 & TWRP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Put the tablet back into Fastboot mode:
     adb reboot bootloader
2. On Windows:
   Double-click "flash_rom.bat" to automatically flash recovery, boot, and system.

   On Linux/macOS:
   Run: chmod +x flash_rom.sh && ./flash_rom.sh

   Or manually run these commands:
     fastboot flash recovery recovery.img
     fastboot flash boot boot.img
     fastboot flash system system.img

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: FORMAT DATA IN TWRP (MANDATORY TO REMOVE STOCK ENCRYPTION)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. From Fastboot, boot directly into TWRP Recovery:
     fastboot reboot recovery
   (Or power off the tablet and hold Power + Volume Up until TWRP opens).
2. Swipe the slider at the bottom to allow modifications.
3. Tap "Wipe" -> tap "Format Data" -> type "yes" and tap the checkmark.
4. Tap back -> tap "Advanced Wipe" -> select "Dalvik / ART Cache" and "Cache" -> swipe to wipe.
5. Tap "Reboot" -> tap "System".

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4: FIRST BOOT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- The first boot takes 2 to 3 minutes while the system compiles bytecode.
- You will be greeted by the LineageOS Setup Wizard!
- Performance optimizations (0.5x animations, 4GB virtual swap, deadline scheduler)
  will activate automatically. Enjoy your clean, fast tablet!
================================================================================
